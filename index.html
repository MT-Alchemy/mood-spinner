    Spin the Emotion Wheel

    Upload your photo to your Instagram Story with a short description (1–3 sentences) explaining your selected emotion and the story behind your photo. Please set your Instagram to a public account.

    Tag MT Alchemy's official Instagram account and at least 3 friends in your Instagram Story.

    Stand a Chance to Win

    Complete all the required steps to qualify for a chance to win prizes worth RM760!
